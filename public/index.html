Aarav, 15, shares one phone with his sister. The video lesson buffers, the chapter feels endless, and nobody notices he stopped reading on page two. He scrolls reels instead.

7:42 pm, with Nova

Nova asks how he feels ("bored"), how he learns ("tell me a story"), and what his setup is ("slow internet"). It switches to Lite mode and rebuilds photosynthesis as a story about a kitchen inside a leaf.

7:55 pm, after

Built for every learner, not the average one

💜 Reads the room

Bored, tired, stressed or curious — the lesson's length, tone and order change with how you feel.

🧭 One-to-one check-ins

Nova notices when you go idle, skim, or switch tabs, and asks what's not clicking instead of pushing on.

🔄 Teaches it another way

Stuck? Switch to a story, a diagram, steps, or a hands-on activity in one tap.

🔊 Voice, only if you want it

Nova can speak and listen. One button turns it on or off, anytime.

📶 Works on slow internet

Built-in topics run fully offline. Lite mode keeps it smooth on old or shared phones.

Difficulty moves with you, hints are there when needed, and results show what Nova noticed.
